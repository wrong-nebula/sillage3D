Drag rocks, cloth, ropes or plants to grab them; release to throw. Scroll while holding to change distance. Drag empty ground or Shift-drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, and scroll or pinch to zoom. WASD or arrow keys move, Shift runs, Space jumps. Alt-click explodes at the pointer; E blasts ahead. B drops a rock, X breaks a nearby rock, H toggles fire, G adds wind, N toggles snowfall. L deposits sand by default, snow in winter; N switches the deposit material with the season. 1 selects snow, 2 sand, 3 mud for a manual override. V views cloth and ropes, R resets them, P views the pond, U drops a rock in water. C clears deposits. K pauses or resumes; F enters fullscreen. Escape releases a held object.
